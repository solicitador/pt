Legal updates

Relevant legislative changes

News with an impact on real estate and businesses in Portugal.

May 2026

Reduced 6% VAT on residential construction

Temporary application of the reduced VAT rate to construction and renovation works on residential property, under the 2026 housing package.

 2026

New IMT brackets and non-resident rate

Update of the IMT (property transfer tax) brackets by around 2% and introduction of a flat 7.5% rate for non-resident buyers in Portugal.

 2026

Capital gains exemption on reinvestment in rental housing

Exemption from personal income tax on real estate capital gains when reinvested in property intended for residential rental.

 Jan 2026

New company size criteria

Decree-Law 126-B/2025 updates the size criteria for companies (micro, small, medium and large) and introduces the concept of medium-sized groups, in force since January 2026.
Frequently asked questions

Common questions

What is a solicitadora in Portugal?+

A solicitadora is a licensed Portuguese legal professional who provides legal support to individuals and businesses: preparing and authenticating documents, handling property deeds and land or commercial registrations, incorporating companies, drafting powers of attorney and dealing with registries, notaries and public authorities in Portugal.

Can you help me buy property in Portugal?+

Yes. I handle the full legal side of a property purchase in Portugal — due diligence on the property, promissory contracts, the deed, tax matters such as IMT, and registration with the land registry — keeping you informed in clear English at every stage.

Can everything be handled remotely?+

In most cases, yes. Consultations take place by video call and most documents circulate digitally. With a power of attorney, many acts can be completed without you travelling to Portugal — a practical solution for clients living abroad.

How are fees set?+

Before any work begins, you receive a clear proposal with the required steps, expected timelines and the costs involved — including registration fees and applicable taxes. No surprises at the end.

Do I need a solicitor or a lawyer (advogado)?+

For deeds, registrations, company incorporation, document authentication and administrative procedures, a solicitadora is usually the most direct option. If your case requires acts reserved to an advogado (attorney), I will tell you transparently and refer you appropriately.
